### Conclusion
- Does: Gives the owner a ranked list of shares that meet the chosen swing-trading rules today, with entry, stop, target and quantity sized to the owner's capital and risk percentage, saved as a CSV file.
- Rules: Defaults: risk 1.5% of capital per trade, target at 2 times the stop distance (`screener.py:703`, `risk.py:91`); stop no wider than 8% of entry (`filters.py:93`); close above the 200-day average (`strategies.py:37`). Entry conditions per strategy at `strategies.py:194` and `strategies.py:261`.
- On failure: Skips symbols without data; falls back to the cached index list; exits on a bad meta-model file; writes no CSV when no candidate qualifies (`screener.py:357`).
- Test result: tested on the surface
- Tests check: Ranking order, no-lookahead and valid stops for both strategies, pullback-length limits, and the `--meta filter` screen run (prints "Meta-model loaded").

## Supporting code

- `candidate_engine.py`: runs strategies over symbols, sizes with the risk engine, ranks and builds the one-line thesis (`candidate_engine.py:196`).
- `strategies.py`: two strategies, `pullback_trend` and `breakout`, registered in `STRATEGIES` (`strategies.py:278`); `TechnicalFilters` adds liquidity, 200-day average and 52-week-high gates.
- `risk.py`: position size, target price, portfolio heat, and `undersized` check.
- `filters.py`: pre-trade filters (liquidity, stop width, results calendar).
- `market.py`: benchmark regime check and 63-day relative strength.
- `universe.py`: downloads NSE index lists, caches for one day, keeps dated snapshots.
- `data.py`: downloads price history with `yfinance` and adds indicator columns.
