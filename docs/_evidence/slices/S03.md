### decision
State: observed
- Summary: The verdict is the first rule that applies, in this order: stop already touched, target already touched, trend broken, time-stop reached, raise the stop, otherwise hold. The trend check for the default strategy is three yes/no tests: close above the 20-day average, close above the 50-day average, 50-day average rising. The stop moves to the entry price once the position is up one unit of initial risk, then follows the lowest low of the last 10 days if that is below the price and above the stop. The time-stop fires at 14 calendar days or more with less than 0.5 units of risk gained.

### Conclusion
- Does: Tells the owner whether an open position still fits the trend rules, whether the stop or target was touched, whether to raise the stop, and whether the position has stalled.
- Rules: Verdict order above (`monitor.py:84-96`); breakeven and trail after +1R (`exits.py:27-30`); time-stop 14 calendar days and 0.5R (`exits.py:36-37`).
- On failure: Prints "Error: ..." and returns without a verdict (`screener.py:635`).
- Test result: tested on the surface
- Tests check: The shared exit functions through backtests (stop, trail, time-stop). Nothing checks the monitor's own verdict order, stop-touched test or error path.
- Known gaps: `PositionMonitor.check`, the verdict ladder, `target_already_hit`, and the behaviour with a raised stop. Search boundary: `PositionMonitor|cmd_monitor|"monitor"|monitor\.check` in all root `*.py` files: matches only in `monitor.py` and `screener.py`; no `test_*.py` file matches.
