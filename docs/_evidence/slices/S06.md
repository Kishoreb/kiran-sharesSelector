### decision
State: observed
- Summary: Entries and exits use only data up to the current bar (no lookahead). Exits are gap-aware: if the day opens through the stop, the fill is the open, not the stop price; if both stop and target fall inside the day's range, the stop is assumed to hit first. The stop trails to breakeven once price is up one unit of initial risk, then up to the recent swing low, using the same `suggest_stop` code the live monitor uses. A position is time-stopped after 14 calendar days with less than 0.5R of progress. At fill time the position is sized against current equity (not free cash), and the portfolio heat ceiling (8%) and maximum position count (5) are enforced here, unlike in `screen` (see S01 U07). Statutory costs and slippage are deducted from every market fill.

### Conclusion
- Does: Simulates what a trader following the chosen strategy and risk rules would have done over the downloaded history, day by day with no lookahead, and reports the resulting trades, equity curve and summary statistics so the owner can judge the rules before risking money.
- Rules: Gap-aware exits at the open; stop assumed to hit before target inside a bar (`backtester.py:288`). Breakeven trail at +1R, then to the recent swing low (`exits.py:27`). Time-stop after 14 calendar days below 0.5R (`exits.py:37`). Equity-based sizing with heat ceiling 8% and max 5 positions enforced at fill time (`backtester.py:263`). Delivery costs and 0.1% slippage per market fill (`costs.py`), switched off by `--no-costs`.
- On failure: Exits with code 1 when no data is fetched (`screener.py:410`); otherwise counts and skips each untradeable signal by reason and closes open positions at the final bar (`backtester.py:354`).
- Test result: well tested
- Tests check: Exit price, reason, date and quantity for gap-through-stop, time-stop and breakeven-trail cases; target gap fills; cost reconciliation against final equity; equity-based sizing; each skip reason; no-lookahead for both strategies and stop modes; point-in-time membership skips.

## Supporting code

- `backtester.py`: `Backtester.run` is the day-by-day simulation loop; `Trade` and `BacktestResult` hold the output; `BacktestResult.metrics()` computes the summary statistics (`backtester.py:85`).
- `costs.py`: `CostModel` computes delivery brokerage, STT, exchange and SEBI charges, GST, stamp duty and the per-sell DP charge, plus a slippage adjustment on market fills; `CostModel.zero()` turns them all off.
- `exits.py`: `suggest_stop` (breakeven-then-swing-low trail) and `time_stop_hit`, shared by the backtester and the live monitor (S03).
- `validation.py`: `make_runner` builds the `Backtester` with parameters merged over `DEFAULT_BASE`, re-using already-downloaded data; also the home of the validation tools read in S07.
