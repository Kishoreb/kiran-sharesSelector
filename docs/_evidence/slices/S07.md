### decision
State: observed
- Summary: Each test turns numbers into a verdict by fixed thresholds. Split: "did not carry over" when out-of-sample average R is not positive while in-sample is; "material degradation" when out-of-sample is below half of in-sample; a minimum of 15 trades in each half or it declines to judge. Sweep: counts how many parameter sets with 10+ trades are profitable, and calls the result robust (80%+), mixed (50-80%) or fragile (below 50%); parameters that give identical results at every value are flagged as inert. Walk-forward: chooses, per window, the parameter set with the best average R over trades closed inside the training window, scores them on the next window, and reports efficiency (out-of-sample avg R / training avg R). Monte Carlo: shuffle and bootstrap drawdown percentiles, and the chance of finishing below the start. A sweep that would try more than `--max-combos` (default 60) combinations is refused.
